Internship Tips for IT Students in Sri Lanka

1. Start applying early, especially before peak internship seasons.
2. Check university career office notices and faculty emails.
3. Use LinkedIn to follow local software companies and startups.
4. Prepare both a CV and a short cover letter.
5. Be ready for online assessments and Zoom interviews.
6. Highlight academic projects if work experience is limited.
7. Mention teamwork, hackathons, open-source, or volunteer tech work.
8. Learn practical tools used by local companies: Git, APIs, SQL, cloud basics.
9. Keep communication professional in email and WhatsApp if recruiters contact you.
10. Ask about internship mode: on-site, hybrid, or remote.

Useful preparation:
- One strong project demo
- GitHub link
- Clear CV tailored to software, QA, data, or IT support roles
