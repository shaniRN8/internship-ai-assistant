LinkedIn Profile Guide for Internship Seekers

1. Use a clear professional photo.
2. Write a headline like: IT Undergraduate | Python | Web Development | Seeking Internship.
3. Add a short About section with your interests, skills, and goals.
4. List education with degree, university, and expected graduation year.
5. Add projects with tech stack and GitHub links.
6. Include skills: Python, Java, SQL, HTML, CSS, Git, communication.
7. Request recommendations from lecturers or teammates if possible.
8. Post or share learning updates occasionally.
9. Follow companies and employees in your target field.
10. Make sure contact info and portfolio links are easy to find.
