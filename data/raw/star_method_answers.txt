STAR Method for Internship Interview Answers

STAR means:
- Situation: brief background
- Task: what needed to be done
- Action: what you personally did
- Result: outcome and learning

Example:
Question: Tell me about a project challenge.
Situation: In a group web project, the login feature failed near the deadline.
Task: I needed to fix authentication and keep the project on schedule.
Action: I reproduced the bug, reviewed the auth flow, fixed token handling, and tested edge cases.
Result: Login worked reliably, we submitted on time, and I learned better debugging habits.

Tips:
- Keep Situation short.
- Make Action the longest part.
- Always include a result.
- Use real student project examples.
