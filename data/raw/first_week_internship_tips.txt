First Week Internship Tips

Day 1 to Day 3:
- Complete onboarding and access setup
- Meet your mentor and teammates
- Learn the project structure and tools
- Read documentation before changing code

Good habits:
- Take notes in every meeting
- Clarify expectations for tasks
- Ask how success is measured
- Start with small tickets
- Introduce yourself politely to the team

Avoid:
- Staying silent when blocked
- Pushing code without review
- Promising deadlines you cannot meet

Goal of week one:
Understand the environment, ship something small, and build trust.
