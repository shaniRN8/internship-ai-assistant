Common CV Mistakes Internship Applicants Should Avoid

1. Too long CV. For students, one page is usually best.
2. No clear contact details or broken links.
3. Writing duties only, without achievements or impact.
4. Listing skills you cannot explain in an interview.
5. Spelling and grammar errors.
6. Unprofessional email address.
7. Dense paragraphs instead of bullet points.
8. Missing projects section.
9. Using one generic CV for every company.
10. Including irrelevant personal details.

Better approach:
- Keep layout clean.
- Put strongest projects near the top.
- Tailor skills and project highlights to the role.
- Quantify results when possible.
