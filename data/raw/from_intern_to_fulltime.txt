How Interns Improve Chances of a Full-Time Offer

1. Treat the internship like a real job, not a temporary class.
2. Deliver tasks on time and communicate blockers early.
3. Ask for feedback and apply it quickly.
4. Document your work and share progress.
5. Volunteer for useful tasks after finishing assigned work.
6. Build good relationships with your mentor and teammates.
7. Write clean code and welcome code review comments.
8. Learn the business context, not only the code.
9. Keep a weekly list of achievements.
10. Near the end, express interest in full-time opportunities professionally.

What managers notice:
- Reliability
- Coachability
- Communication
- Ownership
- Positive attitude
