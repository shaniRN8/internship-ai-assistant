Behavioral Interview Questions for Interns

Common questions:
1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why do you want this internship?
3. Tell me about a team project.
4. Describe a challenge you faced and how you solved it.
5. How do you handle deadlines?
6. Tell me about a time you made a mistake.
7. How do you handle feedback?
8. What are your strengths and weaknesses?
9. Give an example of leadership or initiative.
10. Where do you see yourself in 2 to 3 years?

Answer tips:
- Keep answers structured and specific.
- Use real examples from university projects, clubs, or part-time work.
- Focus on actions you took and what you learned.
- Keep each answer around 1 to 2 minutes.
