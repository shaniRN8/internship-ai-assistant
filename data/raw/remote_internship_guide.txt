Remote Internship Guide for Students

1. Set up a quiet workspace and stable internet.
2. Learn the company tools quickly: Slack, Teams, Jira, GitHub, Notion, etc.
3. Keep your camera-ready for meetings when required.
4. Write clear daily or weekly updates.
5. Ask questions early instead of staying blocked.
6. Manage time zones and meeting schedules carefully.
7. Over-communicate progress on tasks.
8. Keep code, notes, and files organized.
9. Be responsive during agreed working hours.
10. Build trust by delivering small tasks reliably.

Remote intern tip:
Document what you did, what is next, and what blockers you have.
