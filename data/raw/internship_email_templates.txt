Internship Email Templates

Subject examples:
- Application for Software Engineering Internship
- Inquiry About Internship Opportunities - IT Undergraduate
- Follow-up on Internship Application

Short application email structure:
1. Greeting
2. Who you are and your university/year
3. Why you are interested in the company
4. Key skills and one project highlight
5. Attachment note: CV and cover letter
6. Polite closing

Tips:
- Keep the email short and professional.
- Use a formal email address.
- Name files clearly: FirstName_LastName_CV.pdf
- Proofread before sending.
- Follow up once after 7 to 10 days if there is no reply.
