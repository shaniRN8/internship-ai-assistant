Networking Tips for Finding Internships

1. Optimize LinkedIn before sending messages.
2. Connect with alumni from your university.
3. Attend career fairs, meetups, and faculty events.
4. Ask seniors about their internship path.
5. Send short personalized connection messages.
6. Do not start with "Can you get me a job?"
7. Ask for advice or information first.
8. Keep a respectful and grateful tone.
9. Share your CV only when asked or when relevant.
10. Maintain relationships even after the internship search.

Sample message idea:
Hello, I am an IT undergraduate interested in software engineering internships. I admired your path at your company. If you have 5 minutes, I would appreciate any advice on preparing for junior roles.
