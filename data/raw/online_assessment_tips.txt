Online Assessment Tips for IT Internships

Common assessment types:
- Aptitude questions
- Coding challenges
- Debugging tasks
- SQL questions
- Personality or work-style quizzes

Preparation tips:
1. Practice timed coding problems.
2. Revise arrays, strings, loops, and conditionals.
3. Check your internet and power setup before the test.
4. Read instructions fully before starting.
5. Manage time. Do not spend too long on one question.
6. Write clean and readable code.
7. Test with sample cases.
8. Avoid switching tabs if proctoring is enabled.
9. Stay calm if a question looks unfamiliar.
10. Submit a partial working solution rather than nothing.
